# Guard / Vendor / Clinic Worker — silhouette revision 02 construction cards

**Status:** focused directional-construction revision committed for owner review; NOT APPROVED. This remains a design-only package. No renderer/game code, production colour art, QA baseline or golden snapshots changed.

## Revision history and retained direction

Revision 01 was returned because all three figures shared an upright mannequin, similar torso/limb construction and planted stance. Revision 02 established three distinct directions, which are preserved:
- Guard: broad protective wedge, braced forearm, flexed offset knee and heavy boot base.
- Vendor: forward customer pitch, rotated shoulder line, reaching/support arm, open waistcoat negative space and advanced leading foot.
- Clinic Worker: narrow tapered torso, shifted hip, angled feet, asymmetric wrap tunic and separated pouch/hand relationship.

The focused follow-up replaces the repeated/mirrored directional placements with twelve separately authored silhouette constructions: front, back, left profile and right profile for each character. It does not reopen the overall concept direction.

## Guard — protective wedge / articulated braced stance

- Personality: observant, dependable, quietly authoritative.
- Body/pose: compact broad wedge, squared shoulders, short protective shell, chest segmentation, belt break and split hem. One forearm bends across lower chest; weight is uneven over a flexed knee.
- Face: low helmet/brow/nape, broad squared jaw, close-set eyes, straight heavy brow, firm nose bridge and compact chin.
- Face comparisons: Referee (rounder lower face) and Coach (softer cheek/chin); Guard has wider jaw corners and closer eye spacing.
- Shell proposal: protective wedge / articulated braced stance.
- Palette proposal: blue-charcoal #243244, slate #111A25, steel #596B7E, restrained ochre #B38B50.
- Exactly three must-survive: (1) helmet brow/nape and shoulder wedge (direct 28×42 head-read cue); (2) short shell with chest segmentation, belt break and split hem; (3) character-right bracer integrated into bent arm.
- Directional construction: front exposes helmet brow, shoulder wedge, chest segmentation and character-right bracer (viewer-left); back exposes rear helmet shell, nape guard and rear shoulder plates; the chest radio is front-mounted and hidden from the rear; left profile exposes the near-side bent elbow/bracer; right profile occludes the far-side bracer behind the torso rather than duplicating it on the outline.
- Equipment contact: bracer is integrated into the bent forearm; radio is clipped to the chest/gear harness. No handheld baton is proposed.

## Vendor — forward-leaning stall-service / tray-presenting stance

- Personality: approachable, quick-witted, practical and welcoming.
- Body/pose: forward customer pitch, rotated shoulder line, two waistcoat panels with visible shirt-shaped opening, narrow offset sash, advanced leading foot; opposite arm hangs separately.
- Face: soft sloped cap, fuller cheeks, lifted asymmetric brow, gently sloped nose, rounded chin and welcoming asymmetric smile.
- Face comparisons: Cook (more compact lower-face mass) and Courier (narrower cheek/jaw); Vendor has fuller cheeks, wider eye spacing and more expressive mouth.
- Shell proposal: forward-leaning stall-service / tray-presenting stance.
- Palette proposal: berry #4B3048, shadow #261B2B, linen #D6C7A7, saffron #D8A247.
- Exactly three must-survive: (1) cap slope and cheek contour (direct 28×42 head-read cue); (2) open waistcoat with shirt negative space and offset sash; (3) forward gesture with shallow tray and wrist/palm support.
- Directional construction: front shows the forward pitch, split waistcoat and tray on character-left (viewer-right); back shows the rear vest panels, neck/sash construction and tray carried on the far side, mostly occluded; left profile shows the near-side arm chain and palm under the tray lip; right profile shows far-side tray/hand occlusion and a distinct side-on torso/leg pitch.
- Prop contact: tray character-left; continuous shoulder → upper arm → forearm → wrist → palm under the tray lip in the front and near-side profile. The far-side and rear views show occlusion, not a second floating hand.

## Clinic Worker — tapered wrap tunic / offset-hip stance

- Personality: calm, attentive, reassuring and capable.
- Body/pose: narrow tapered torso, softer shoulder line, weight shifted over one hip, angled step, short curved tunic exposing trousers, diagonal wrap overlap.
- Face/hair: swept-back hair gathered into a low rear bun/coil and side tie; tapered jaw, wider-set eyes, raised inner brow and shallow nose bridge.
- Face comparisons: Usher (more vertically formal narrow face) and Courier (narrower lower cheek); Clinic has rounder chin and wider eye spacing.
- Shell proposal: tapered wrap tunic / offset-hip stance.
- Palette proposal: teal #285D5B, shadow #173C3C, sea-glass #B5D5C9, muted plum #8B647F.
- Exactly three must-survive: (1) gathered low hair and side tie (direct 28×42 head-read cue); (2) short asymmetric wrap tunic with diagonal overlap, curved hem and trouser break; (3) character-left hip pouch with hand kept separate.
- Directional construction: front shows the diagonal wrap overlap and character-left pouch (viewer-right); back shows rear yoke seam, gathered coil/side tie and pouch on rear viewer-left; left profile exposes near-side hair gathering and tunic overlap with pouch behind the torso edge; right profile hides the far-side pouch while retaining the shifted hip and curved hem.
- Prop/contact: pouch is attached to the character-left hip. The hand remains visibly separate with negative space; this is not a handheld kit or grip.
