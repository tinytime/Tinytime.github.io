# Guard / Vendor / Clinic Worker — silhouette revision 02

**Status:** design exploration returned for owner review; NOT APPROVED. No renderer/game code, production colour art, QA baseline or golden snapshots changed.

## Response to review
Revision 01 was returned because all three shared an upright mannequin, similar torso/limb construction and planted stance. Revision 02 changes body silhouette and weight distribution first:
- Guard: broad protective upper wedge, braced forearm, flexed offset knee and heavy boot base.
- Vendor: forward customer pitch, rotated shoulder line, reaching/support arm and advanced leading foot; open vest uses a shirt-shaped negative-space opening.
- Clinic Worker: narrow tapered torso, shifted hip, angled feet and wrap-front construction; pouch and hand are separated.

## Guard — protective wedge / articulated braced stance
- Personality: observant, dependable, quietly authoritative.
- Body/pose: compact broad wedge, squared shoulders, short protective shell, chest segmentation, belt break and split hem. One forearm bends across lower chest; weight is uneven over a flexed knee.
- Face: low helmet/brow/nape, broad squared jaw, close-set eyes, straight heavy brow, firm nose bridge and compact chin.
- Face comparisons: Referee (rounder lower face) and Coach (softer cheek/chin); Guard has wider jaw corners and closer eye spacing.
- Shell proposal: protective wedge / articulated braced stance.
- Palette proposal: blue-charcoal #243244, slate #111A25, steel #596B7E, restrained ochre #B38B50.
- Exactly three must-survive: (1) helmet brow/nape and shoulder wedge (28×42 head cue); (2) short shell with chest segmentation, belt break and split hem; (3) character-right bracer integrated into bent arm.
- Contact: bracer character-right (front viewer-left, rear viewer-right), visible left profile and occluded right profile. Radio is clipped to chest; no handheld baton.

## Vendor — forward-leaning stall-service / tray-presenting stance
- Personality: approachable, quick-witted, practical and welcoming.
- Body/pose: forward customer pitch, rotated shoulder line, two waistcoat panels with visible shirt-shaped opening, narrow offset sash, advanced leading foot; opposite arm hangs separately.
- Face: soft sloped cap, fuller cheeks, lifted asymmetric brow, gently sloped nose, rounded chin and welcoming asymmetric smile.
- Face comparisons: Cook (more compact lower-face mass) and Courier (narrower cheek/jaw); Vendor has fuller cheeks, wider eye spacing and more expressive mouth.
- Shell proposal: forward-leaning stall-service / tray-presenting stance.
- Palette proposal: berry #4B3048, shadow #261B2B, linen #D6C7A7, saffron #D8A247.
- Exactly three must-survive: (1) cap slope and cheek contour (28×42 head cue); (2) open waistcoat with shirt negative space and offset sash; (3) forward gesture with shallow tray and wrist-to-palm support.
- Contact: tray character-left (front viewer-right), continuous shoulder→upper arm→forearm→wrist→palm beneath tray lip. Rear tray is occluded; left profile near-side contact, right profile far-side occlusion.

## Clinic Worker — tapered wrap tunic / offset-hip stance
- Personality: calm, attentive, reassuring and capable.
- Body/pose: narrow tapered torso, softer shoulder line, weight shifted over one hip, angled step, short curved tunic exposing trousers, diagonal wrap overlap.
- Face: swept-back hair gathered into low rear bun/coil and side tie; tapered jaw, wider-set eyes, raised inner brow and shallow nose bridge.
- Face comparisons: Usher (more vertically formal narrow face) and Courier (narrower lower cheek); Clinic has rounder chin and wider eye spacing.
- Shell proposal: tapered wrap tunic / offset-hip stance.
- Palette proposal: teal #285D5B, shadow #173C3C, sea-glass #B5D5C9, muted plum #8B647F.
- Exactly three must-survive: (1) gathered low hair and side tie (28×42 head cue); (2) short asymmetric wrap tunic with diagonal overlap, curved hem and trouser break; (3) character-left hip pouch with hand kept separate.
- Contact: pouch character-left (front viewer-right; rear viewer-left); hand remains separated by a gap, not a handheld kit.
