**Scope:** design only. No renderer/game code, production colour art, QA baseline, golden snapshots or approval states changed.  
**Construction field:** matched 64×96. The companion SVG includes four views, front/profile face studies and actual 28×42 raster downsamplings from the exploratory silhouettes.

## What changed in revision 02

The first exploration was returned because the three figures shared too much of the same mannequin: similar torso width, straight hanging arms, equal-weight feet and upright weight distribution. Revision 02 changes the body/pose construction first:

- **Guard:** broader upper wedge, asymmetrical braced forearm, offset bent-knee base and heavier protective boot placement.
- **Vendor:** forward customer pitch, rotated shoulder line, reaching/support arm, a visibly advanced leading foot and a narrow open-vest torso with a shirt-shaped negative-space opening.
- **Clinic Worker:** narrower tapered torso, shifted hip, differently angled feet, short wrap construction and a pouch/hand relationship separated by a visible gap.

These are proposed construction families, not numeric anatomy targets. No proportion bands, score floors, IoU targets or family quotas are introduced.

## 1. Guard — security / protective role

**Personality:** observant, dependable and quietly authoritative; alert rather than aggressive.

### Body and silhouette construction

- A compact, broad protective wedge rather than a vertical uniform column.
- Low helmet with a forward brow shelf and rear nape contour.
- Squared shoulder corners lead into a short protective overshirt; the chest plane is visibly segmented, with a clear belt break and short lower edge.
- The character-right forearm carries a broad, separate bracer plate. The arm is bent and braced across the lower chest rather than hanging parallel to the torso.
- Legs are not equal vertical columns: one knee is flexed and the weight is set unevenly. Broad work boots give the stance a stable, ready-to-move base.
- Back view keeps the protective shoulder/back plate and the bracer on the correct rear-view side.

### Face construction study

- Broad lower jaw with a compact, squared chin.
- Heavier, straighter brow and relatively close-set eyes.
- Firm nose bridge and a more angular nose-to-chin profile break.
- Expression: focused and attentive, with a neutral mouth rather than a scowl.

**Closest existing face comparisons (geometry only):**
- **Referee:** both can read as focused and compact. Guard's jaw is broader/squarer, brow heavier and closer to the eye line; Referee's lower face is more vertically rounded.
- **Coach:** both have a firm brow. Guard uses closer eye spacing and a harder jaw corner; Coach's face is softer and more open through the cheek/chin.
- Exclude helmet, skin tone, hair colour and palette from this comparison. These are preliminary design comparisons; final matched face-crop review remains a production-stage check.

### Proposed shell family

**Protective wedge / articulated braced stance.** This is not a Coach yoke or Usher coat: the shoulder mass is armour-led, the forearm is a distinct braced plane, and the lower shell ends above the belt/hip break.

### Palette proposal

- Main protective fabric: deep blue-charcoal `#243244`
- Recessed shadow: near-black slate `#111A25`
- Armour / overshirt planes: desaturated steel `#596B7E`
- Restrained equipment accent: muted ochre `#B38B50`
- Keep the accent small; it must not become a glowing stripe or a substitute for the silhouette.

### Exactly three must-survive items

1. Low helmet brow plus rear nape and squared shoulder wedge (**direct 28×42 head-read cue: low helmet brow/nape profile**).
2. Short protective shell with visible chest segmentation, belt break and split hem.
3. Character-right bracer integrated with the bent arm; the protective role must still read without a baton.

### Prop, contact and handedness record

| View | Equipment/contact |
|---|---|
| Front | Bracer on character-right = viewer-left. The bracer is part of the near forearm contour. Compact radio is clipped to the upper chest; no handheld prop. |
| Back | Bracer on character-right = viewer-right. Radio is occluded by the torso and is correctly absent. |
| Left profile | Near-side bracer remains visible on the single visible arm. Far arm is occluded by the torso. |
| Right profile | Bracer is on the far side and is occluded; do not duplicate it on the near arm. The single near arm remains anatomically clear. |

No baton is included. If a handheld prop is proposed later, it requires a new owner-reviewed contact study before production.

### 28×42 reduction

The sheet includes front and near-side-profile 28×42 raster downsamplings. The helmet contour, broad shoulder wedge, bent bracer arm and offset stance are intended to remain. Radio buttons, rivets, seams and tiny belt hardware may disappear.

## 2. Vendor — market / stall worker

**Personality:** approachable, quick-witted, practical and welcoming; confident with regular customers.

### Body and silhouette construction

- A forward-pitched customer-facing torso, not a neutral upright mannequin.
- One shoulder drops and rotates forward toward the tray; the waist and torso lean into the interaction.
- A shallow tray is carried in front of the body by a clearly connected bent arm. The support wrist continues into the palm beneath the tray edge.
- The opposite arm hangs in a relaxed, separately readable shape.
- One foot advances toward the customer; the trailing leg remains behind the hip, giving the figure a stepped base rather than two equally planted feet.
- The open waistcoat has two distinct front panels with a broad shirt-shaped negative-space opening between them. A narrow offset sash crosses the waist; there is no broad apron wedge.
- Rear view has a short waistcoat back and tied sash rhythm; the tray is occluded by the body.

### Face construction study

- Rounded cheek volume and a softer, fuller lower face.
- Outer brow lifts asymmetrically; the eyes are slightly more open than Guard's.
- Nose bridge slopes gently and transitions into a rounded chin.
- A small asymmetrical smile gives the face a welcoming, familiar-customer expression.

**Closest existing face comparisons (geometry only):**
- **Cook:** both have fuller cheek volume. Vendor's jaw is rounder and less broad at the chin; the brow lifts asymmetrically and the mouth has a clearer smile. Cook's expression and lower-face mass are more compact.
- **Courier:** both use a softer nose/cheek transition. Vendor's cheeks are fuller, eye spacing slightly wider and mouth more expressive; Courier's face is narrower through the lower cheek/jaw.
- Exclude hat, hair, skin tone and palette from this comparison. Final matched face-crop review remains a production-stage check.

### Proposed shell family

**Forward-leaning stall-service / tray-presenting stance.** The identity comes from the shoulder rotation, torso pitch, stepped feet, open waistcoat and arm-to-tray contact—not from a hat or tray alone.

### Palette proposal

- Waistcoat: muted berry/plum `#4B3048`
- Deep vest shadow: `#261B2B`
- Rolled-sleeve shirt: warm natural linen `#D6C7A7`
- Small market accent: saffron/ochre `#D8A247`
- Avoid Cook's copper/chef warmth, Courier's orange/cyan and Usher's navy/brass. Keep large material planes distinct at game scale.

### Exactly three must-survive items

1. Sloped soft market cap with rounded cheek contour (**direct 28×42 head-read cue: cap slope and cheek mass**).
2. Open short waistcoat with visible shirt negative space and a narrow offset sash.
3. Forward customer gesture with shallow tray and continuous wrist-to-palm support beneath the tray lip.

### Prop, contact and handedness record

| View | Equipment/contact |
|---|---|
| Front | Tray is held forward by character-left = viewer-right. The bent arm runs shoulder → upper arm → forearm → wrist → palm; the palm supports the tray underside. The opposite arm hangs separately. |
| Back | Tray and support hand are occluded by the torso; they are intentionally absent. Waistcoat back and sash remain visible. |
| Left profile | Near-side support arm, palm and tray edge are visible as one continuous contact chain. The far arm is occluded. |
| Right profile | Tray/support arm are on the far side and are occluded; do not mirror a second tray onto the near arm. The near arm is the only visible arm. |

The tray is a shallow market tray with no pan handle. Do not add produce pieces until they can remain subordinate to the tray silhouette.

### 28×42 reduction

The sheet includes front and near-side-profile 28×42 raster downsamplings. Preserve the cap slope, shirt-shaped vest opening, forward lean, advanced foot and tray/support block. Buttons, tiny produce, stitching and cloth folds may disappear.

## 3. Clinic Worker — healthcare role

**Personality:** calm, attentive, reassuring and capable; clinical without feeling like a generic lab scientist.

### Body and silhouette construction

- A narrower, tapered torso rather than the broad protective or forward-leaning market body.
- The waist shifts over one hip; the shoulder line is softer and slightly sloped.
- One leg carries the weight while the other steps out at an angle; the feet are not a matched pair of straight planted boots.
- A short tunic ends above the knee and exposes a clear trouser break. The lower edge is softly curved rather than a straight jacket hem.
- A diagonal wrap flap crosses the torso and creates a visible overlap/negative-space edge. It must remain a garment closure, not read as a bib or apron.
- The low gathered hair mass and bun/coil extend behind the head. The side tie is secondary; the gathered rear mass is the head-read.
- The care pouch is attached at the character-left hip. The hand rests near it but is separated from the pouch by a visible gap; it does not fuse into one black shape.

### Face construction study

- Softer tapered jaw and rounded chin, narrower than Vendor's cheek mass and less squared than Guard's.
- Wider-set eyes with a gently raised inner brow.
- Shallow nose bridge with a clear forehead-to-nose transition.
- Calm, reassuring expression with a small, relaxed mouth.

**Closest existing face comparisons (geometry only):**
- **Usher:** both have a composed expression. Clinic Worker has a softer, more tapered jaw and wider eye spacing; Usher's face is narrower and more vertically formal.
- **Courier:** both have a shallow nose bridge. Clinic Worker has a rounder chin, wider-set eyes and a raised inner brow; Courier is narrower through the jaw and cheek.
- Exclude hair, skin tone, headwear and palette. Final matched face-crop review remains a production-stage check.

### Proposed shell family

**Tapered wrap tunic / offset-hip stance.** This is a short asymmetric clinical garment with a curved hem, visible trousers and one shifted hip—not a long lab coat, formal uniform column or broad apron.

### Palette proposal

- Tunic: deep muted teal `#285D5B`
- Recessed shadow: dark green-teal `#173C3C`
- Wrap / fabric plane: pale sea-glass `#B5D5C9`
- Small personal accent: muted plum `#8B647F`
- Avoid an all-white clinical coat. Keep material separation and the darker trouser break visible.

### Exactly three must-survive items

1. Swept-back hair gathered into a low rear bun/coil (**direct 28×42 head-read cue: rear hair gather and side tie**).
2. Short asymmetric wrap tunic with a visible diagonal overlap, curved hem and trouser break.
3. Compact character-left hip care pouch with the hand kept visibly separate.

### Prop, contact and handedness record

| View | Equipment/contact |
|---|---|
| Front | Care pouch on character-left = viewer-right. The hand is near the pouch but separated; wrist/palm and pouch edges remain distinct. |
| Back | Pouch shifts to viewer-left because character-left reverses in the rear view. The hand is occluded by the body and is not duplicated. |
| Left profile | Near-side pouch and adjacent hand are visible; the gap between hand and pouch remains. Far-side arm is occluded. |
| Right profile | Pouch/hand are on the far side and are occluded; the near arm remains a single clean contour. |

The pouch is a fixed wearable prop, not a handheld medical kit. No separate hand-to-kit contact is required.

### 28×42 reduction

The sheet includes front and near-side-profile 28×42 raster downsamplings. Preserve the low gathered hair, diagonal wrap, curved tunic hem, visible trouser break and hip pouch block. Individual hair strands, badge text, pouch contents and fine stitching may disappear.
